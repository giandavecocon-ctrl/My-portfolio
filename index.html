About Me

I'm an IT student who splits my time between hands-on hardware work and photography — right now I'm building an ESP32-based IoT system that uses machine learning to predict flooding and protect rice seedlings, while also using photography as a way to slow down and notice details I'd otherwise miss. What drives me is that same instinct in both: paying close attention to small signals, whether it's a sensor reading or the way light falls on a subject, and using that to build or capture something better. I'm working toward becoming an engineer who brings that same care and attention to bigger, real-world problems.

Background

I'm currently studying Information Technology, where I first got hooked on the idea of using technology to solve real problems — that interest led me to my current thesis project, building an IoT system that helps predict flooding and protect rice crops.

Hobbies
